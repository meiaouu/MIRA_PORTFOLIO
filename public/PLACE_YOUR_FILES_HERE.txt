Add your own assets to this folder:

- resume.pdf                    -> your downloadable resume (referenced in data/portfolio.ts)
- me1.png, me2.png            -> your photos for the About section pop-in card
                                    (me1 shows by default, me2 crossfades in on hover —
                                    see components/AboutPhoto.tsx)
- projects/project-1.jpg, project-2.jpg, project-3.jpg -> project screenshots
  (wire them up in components/Projects.tsx using next/image)

You can delete this file once you've added your own assets.
